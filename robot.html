03 / STANDOUT ANALYSIS

KEY ATTACHMENT MECHANICS

MOST IMPRESSIVE: ROUTE 02

High Efficiency: Yields 70 points in under 20 seconds of execution time.

Dual-Action Capability: Multi-gear setup allows one motor to drive two distinct attachment functions.

Passive Alignment: Relies on mechanical indexing guide structures for precision without software lag.

MOST CHALLENGING: ROUTE 01

Testing Rigor: Required the highest number of iteration cycles due to multi-zone navigation tolerances.

Precision Requirements: Heavily reliant on exact motor encoder readings across long distances.

Wall-Following Integration: Uses a physical roller attachment to ride the field walls and correct drift errors.
